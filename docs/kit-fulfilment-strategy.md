## 2. Supliful Verdict

**Use Supliful for exactly one thing: the US store lane.** There it's genuinely good — no MOQ, $4.50 domestic shipping, 60–80% gross margins, clean Shopify auto-fulfilment, ~$29–49/mo.

Where it does **not** fit:
- **EU: disqualified.** No Germany, Spain, Ireland, Austria, Greece; all fulfilment is US-origin DDU with $20+/lb shipping ([zones](https://help.supliful.com/en/articles/6771560-where-can-the-products-be-shipped)). One third-party page claims Germany shipping — the official zone list wins.
- **UK: workable but don't.** EORI requirement, $20/lb, DDU import-VAT surprise, probable ≤£135 seller-VAT conflict. UK-domestic dropshippers beat it on every axis.
- **AU: prohibited in practice.** Non-ARTG product advertised/sold to AU consumers is a TGA violation, and Supliful explicitly disclaims destination-legality responsibility with no refunds on customs-blocked orders ([intl shipping](https://help.supliful.com/en/articles/8459369-international-shipping)).
- **Kit-composition gaps even in the US:** no standalone L-theanine, glycine, or NR — only blends and NMN ([catalog](https://supliful.com/catalog)).

## 3. Per-Kit Strategy

### Sleep kit (magnesium glycinate, L-theanine, glycine, melatonin US-only)
- **US — store via Supliful, with substitution:** magnesium glycinate available standalone; L-theanine/glycine only exist inside blends — use Supliful's Sleep Support Capsules (melatonin + L-theanine + ashwagandha) as the second SKU, or keep L-theanine/glycine as affiliate line-items (Momentous/Thorne carry both). Melatonin stays US-only per existing gating.
- **EU — store via Suplify** (audit their catalog for magnesium/theanine first; fall back to affiliate: Shop Apotheke). No melatonin.
- **UK — store via Specialist Supplements/Nutribl** (Nutribl lists magnesium and ashwagandha). Affiliate top-up via Holland & Barrett.
- **AU — affiliate only** (Aussie Health Products, Mr Vitamins, iHerb). No melatonin (S4/S3 — pharmacist-only). Note the TGA Feb-2024 ashwagandha liver-injury advisory if a blend contains it ([TGA alert](https://www.tga.gov.au/safety/safety-monitoring-and-information/safety-alerts/medicines-containing-withania-somnifera-withania-ashwagandha)).

### Longevity kit (NR/NMN, omega-3)
- **US — store via Supliful:** NMN 500mg + their single omega-3 SKU. No NR exists — substitute NMN, and flag the FDA NMN gray-zone status on the listing (enforcement discretionary; monitor).
- **EU — affiliate only, no NMN/NR:** NMN is an unauthorized novel food, enforcement pulled longevity ingredients from EU retail in 2025/26 ([nutraingredients](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/02/02/longevity-ingredients-under-the-eu-regulatory-spotlight/)). Ship the kit as omega-3-centric via Shop Apotheke affiliate links; keep NMN blocked (already gated).
- **UK — store omega-3 via UK dropship; NMN/NR via affiliate** (check Nutribl/Specialist Supplements catalogs; UK novel-food status of NMN mirrors EU — treat as affiliate-only or exclude).
- **AU — affiliate only** (iHerb personal-importation for NMN is the consumer's pathway; safer to link ARTG-listed omega-3 at AU retailers and drop NMN from the AU kit).

### Self-Heal herbal kit (Tre Lune partner, AU)
- **AU — partner-cart with Tre Lune** (they are seller-of-record, own compliance; topicals likely sit outside ARTG as cosmetics; negotiated wholesale margin beats affiliate). They ship 150+ countries, so the partner-cart can serve US/UK/EU too — but check destination-country herbal rules per SKU.
- **Due-diligence flag, decide consciously:** Tre Lune's founders were mentored by Barbara O'Neill, who is under a permanent NSW HCCC prohibition order (2019) for non-evidence-based health claims ([HCCC](https://www.hccc.nsw.gov.au/decisions-orders/media-releases/2019/mrs-barbara-o-neill-breaches-of-code-of-conduct-permanent-prohibition-order)). For a health-science-positioned brand this is real association risk. Recommendation: keep the partnership but do not co-brand marketing copy, and vet every product claim on their pages before linking.

### Glass Skin K-beauty kit
- **Affiliate everywhere — beauty rates invert the supplement picture:** Amazon US Beauty 3% / Luxury Beauty 10%; Amazon UK Beauty 4%; **Amazon AU Beauty 8%** (the one place Amazon AU is useful); iHerb 5% carries K-beauty globally. This kit needs no dropship lane at all — cosmetics dropship (Jubilee etc.) adds fulfilment burden for no margin advantage over 8–10% zero-effort affiliate.

## 5. Open Risks — Honest List

1. **Regulatory ownership (highest).** On Supliful/Suplify, HealthScan is brand-holder of record: FDA structure/function notification, claims substantiation, mandatory disclaimer formatting are yours ([Supliful obligations](https://help.supliful.com/en/articles/9545861-entrepreneur-obligations-and-responsibilities)). An independent compliance review flags that Supliful's own catalog copy over-claims — don't copy it ([Blue Ocean Regulatory](https://www.blueoceanregulatory.com/blog/a-review-of-supliful-by-compliance-expert)).
2. **NMN.** US: gray zone (FDA's 2022 exclusion position, discretionary enforcement) — the Longevity kit's anchor SKU could be pulled. EU: unauthorized novel food, actively enforced. Keep the existing EU block; consider dropping NMN from UK/AU kits too.
3. **Suplify is young and unaudited.** It's the linchpin of the entire EU store lane; catalog depth, subscription price, and reliability are all unconfirmed beyond the marketing site. Pilot with real orders before the admin marks EU kits "in stock."
4. **UK dropship claims are the weakest-sourced part of this brief.** Nutribl/Specialist Supplements details were not re-verified by fact-check — confirm MOQ, pricing, and integration before build-out.
5. **UK Amazon rate ambiguity.** The 3% assumption could actually be 1% (Grocery mapping) — verify post-signup before projecting UK affiliate revenue.
6. **Quality/COA visibility.** Supliful offers limited lot-level testing transparency; request ISO 17025 COAs, and expect occasional silent stock-outs (documented 2+ week delays).
7. **Margin compression on multi-item kits.** Even domestically, each extra bottle adds weight-based shipping; internationally it's $6–8/lb — kits must be priced as bundles with shipping baked in.
8. **Tre Lune association risk.** The Barbara O'Neill HCCC prohibition connection is public and searchable; a journalist could connect it to HealthScan. Mitigate with claim-vetting and arm's-length branding, and apply "vet founder/claims history" as a standing partner-cart criterion.
9. **iHerb rates are secondary-sourced.** The 5%/7-day/10%-intro figures come from directories, not iHerb's own page (which redirects) — confirm at application before hard-coding `commission_pct = 5`.

**Bottom line:** Supliful for US store, Suplify for EU store (piloted), UK-domestic dropship for UK store, partner-cart for AU store; affiliate stacks led by Momentous (US 15%), Shop Apotheke (EU 12%), Holland & Barrett (UK ~8%), Aussie Health Products (AU 10%), with iHerb 5% as the universal fallback. Amazon only where nothing else exists — and never for AU supplements.
